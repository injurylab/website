What we study

Three pillars of inquiry

Our interdisciplinary program bridges developmental psychology, computer science, and neuroscience to reduce the burden of childhood injury.

🧠

TBI Rehabilitation

We develop and rigorously test VR-based cognitive rehabilitation programs for children with traumatic brain injury, targeting executive function deficits through randomized controlled trials and fNIRS neuroimaging.

VR-VICTfNIRSExecutive FunctionRCT
📊

Injury Epidemiology

Using HCUP NEDS, MEPS, and other national databases, we examine injury risk, healthcare utilization, and economic burden across the pediatric population — with a focus on neurodivergent youth including children with ADHD and autism spectrum disorder.

HCUP NEDSMEPSNeurodivergent YouthR
🛡️

Injury Prevention

From roadway safety to caregiver education, our prevention research uses technology-assisted approaches — including AR for implicit bias training — to reduce injury incidence in children and adolescents, including those with autism, ADHD, and other neurodevelopmental conditions.

Recent & featured work

Key publications from the lab. Full list on Google Scholar →

Journal of Attention Disorders2023First Author

A Population-Based Study on Injury Risk and Disparities Among Children With ADHD

Shen, J., Shi, J., Gauthier, L., & Li, W.

Using 66,236 children from the Medical Expenditures Panel Survey (MEPS) 2010–2019, this study found children with ADHD have significantly higher injury prevalence (OR = 1.20) across injury types and most sociodemographic subgroups, with disparities by race/ethnicity, income, and insurance status.
