What we study

Three pillars of inquiry

Our interdisciplinary program bridges developmental psychology, computer science, and neuroscience to reduce the burden of childhood injury and improve outcomes for neurodivergent youth.

Injury Prevention

From roadway safety to caregiver education, our prevention research uses technology-assisted approaches — including augmented reality for implicit bias training — to reduce injury incidence in children and adolescents, with particular attention to youth with autism, ADHD, and other neurodevelopmental conditions.

Augmented RealityCaregiver EducationRoadway SafetyPediatric Safety

Neurodiversity

Using HCUP NEDS, MEPS, and other national databases, we examine injury risk, healthcare utilization, and economic burden among neurodivergent youth — including children with autism spectrum disorder and ADHD. Dr. Shen co-directs the Center for Neurodiversity Education, Support and Training (NEST), which anchors this research within a broader university-wide commitment to the autism and neurodivergent community.

HCUP NEDSMEPSAutism & ADHDR

Brain Injury Rehabilitation

We develop and rigorously test VR-based cognitive rehabilitation programs for children with traumatic brain injury, targeting executive function deficits through randomized controlled trials and fNIRS neuroimaging.
