What we study

Three pillars of inquiry

Our interdisciplinary program bridges developmental psychology, computer science, and neuroscience to reduce the burden of childhood injury.

🧠

TBI Rehabilitation

We develop and rigorously test VR-based cognitive rehabilitation programs for children with traumatic brain injury, targeting executive function deficits through randomized controlled trials and fNIRS neuroimaging.

VR-VICTfNIRSExecutive FunctionRCT
📊

Injury Epidemiology

Using HCUP NEDS, LONGSCAN, and other national databases, we examine patterns of childhood injury, risk factors, disparities, and long-term health outcomes across the developmental lifespan.

HCUP NEDSLONGSCANChild MaltreatmentR
🛡️

Injury Prevention

From roadway safety to caregiver education, our prevention research uses technology-assisted approaches — including AR for implicit bias training — to reduce injury incidence in children and adolescents.

Augmented RealityCaregiver EducationPediatric Safety

The people

Meet the lab

A collaborative, interdisciplinary team committed to rigorous science and mentoring the next generation of researchers.

JS
Principal Investigator
Jiabin Shen, PhD
APA Fellow Tenured Associate Professor Co-Director, NEST

Dr. Shen has studied pediatric injury prevention and rehabilitation since 2011. He directs the Pediatric Injury Research Lab at UMass Lowell, where his interdisciplinary program integrates virtual reality, fNIRS, and large-scale national datasets to improve outcomes for children with TBI, ADHD, and autism. Over $1.5M in federal funding secured as PI.
